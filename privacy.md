# Pakiden Confession Bot Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 21, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Pakiden Confession Bot** ("the Bot") processes information when users interact with its confession system.

## 1. Information We Process

When a user submits a confession through the Bot, the Bot may process information including:

* Discord User ID
* Discord Server/Guild ID
* Submitted confession content
* Date and time of the submission
* Relevant Discord channel information
* Other technical information required for the Bot to operate correctly

## 2. Confession Logging

Confessions submitted through the Bot may be logged in a private moderation or logging channel accessible to authorized Pakiden staff.

While confessions may appear anonymous to regular server members, authorized moderators may have access to identifying information for moderation, security, and abuse-prevention purposes.

Users should therefore not assume that submissions are completely anonymous from authorized server staff.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the Bot may be used to:

* Deliver confession submissions
* Operate the confession system
* Prevent spam, harassment, threats, or abuse
* Investigate violations of server rules
* Assist authorized moderators
* Maintain the security and reliability of the Bot

## 4. Data Storage

Information may be stored through Discord messages, moderation logs, or other storage systems necessary for operating the Bot.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for moderation, security, abuse prevention, or operation of the service.

## 5. Data Sharing

Information collected or processed by the Bot is not sold to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Information may be accessible to authorized Pakiden administrators or moderators where necessary for moderation, security, or operation of the service.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or valid legal process.

## 6. User Responsibilities

Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information through the confession system.

Users are responsible for the content they submit through the Bot.

## 7. Data Requests

Users may contact Pakiden server administration to request information regarding data associated with their Discord account or to request deletion where reasonably possible.

Certain information may need to be retained temporarily where necessary for security, abuse prevention, moderation records, or legal obligations.

## 8. Discord

Pakiden Confession Bot operates through Discord.

Use of Discord itself is also governed by Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be available through the Bot's official Privacy Policy page.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, users may contact the **Pakiden Administration** through the official Pakiden Discord server.
