Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy describes how Red X ModGuard (“Bot”) may process information when used in Discord servers or through its associated dashboard, where available. Processing depends on the features enabled and server configuration.

Information that may be processed

  • Discord user, server, channel and role IDs needed to run commands and moderation actions.
  • Command inputs, moderation action types, reasons entered by moderators, timestamps and expiry details where applicable.
  • Server configuration, including enabled moderation settings, custom commands, role/channel mappings and logging settings.
  • Message content when required to recognise commands or operate an enabled feature.
  • Dashboard account/server-access information and settings submitted through the dashboard.
  • Limited technical information needed for security, diagnostics and operation.

How information is used

Information may be used to execute commands, apply and document moderation actions, maintain configuration, prevent abuse, secure the Bot and dashboard, diagnose errors and respond to privacy requests.

Storage and retention

Depending on the feature, information may be stored in the Bot’s data store or database and/or in Discord messages and moderation-log channels. Information is retained as reasonably needed for operation, configuration, moderation, security or applicable obligations. Actual retention varies by feature and server configuration.

Access and sharing

The developer and server administrators or moderators with relevant permissions may be able to access information. Discord and infrastructure providers may process information to provide their services. Information is not sold to advertisers. It may be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to address abuse or protect the service.

Server permissions

Server administrators decide which permissions to grant, features to enable and staff who can access logs or dashboard settings. Grant only permissions needed. A server may retain its own copies of messages or logs after settings change.

Data requests

For access or deletion requests, contact the developer through the support/contact method listed on the Bot’s Discord application page. Deletion may not be possible for data held by Discord or a server, or where retention is needed for security, moderation or legal reasons.

Security and age

Reasonable safeguards may be used, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users must meet Discord’s applicable age requirements and follow Discord’s Terms and Community Guidelines.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as the Bot’s features or practices change. For questions, use the support/contact method listed on the Red X ModGuard Discord application page.
